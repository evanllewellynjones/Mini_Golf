Our base case
18 holes, bar, ice cream, food trucks; closed Mon–Tue
12,500 SF bldg + yard$12$766,350~$61 per bldg SFOur model
  • US mini golf revenue is estimated at over $2 billion a year. About 18 million Americans played in 2021, averaging four rounds each (National Golf Foundation, via Washington Examiner, Jul 2024).
  • A typical Harris-built course takes in $250k–$500k, costs $300k–$2.5M to build, runs with about 2 staff per shift and holds about 90 players at once.
  • Our base case of $766,350 is above the classic outdoor range ($250k–$500k), below the indoor franchise level (~$1.0M) and far below the chains, which run 15–25k SF venues with kitchens.

Where we fit

  • No mini golf venue of any kind appears to operate in Durham now that Wheels Fun Park has closed. Hop Shots in Raleigh is the closest format, and Puttery Raleigh is the nearest chain unit.
  • Price: Stroke charged $10–12 before closing and Puttery charges $18–20. Our base case is $12 a round for everyone.
  • Food trucks replace a kitchen: no kitchen capex, no kitchen labor, but less food revenue than the chains.
  • Family demand is seasonal and weather-driven, and our 9 yard holes are exposed to both.
