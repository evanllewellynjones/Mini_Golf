Where we fit

  • No permanent 18-hole, bar-forward mini golf venue appears to exist in or near downtown Durham. The closest true comp, Puttery, is in Raleigh.
  • Price gap: Bull City Mini charged $7 in 2019 and Puttery charges $20 unlimited. Our base case sits between: $11 daytime, $14 night.
  • Food trucks are the same trick State of Golf uses: no kitchen capex, no kitchen labor.
  • The yard and covered holes give us an outdoor-evening feel the indoor venues can't match.

Risks from the competitive set

  • Topgolf and State of Golf already hold the adult night-out and corporate event budget.
  • The only downtown mini golf precedent did not last. Call its owners for real volume data before committing.
  • Family demand is seasonal and weather-driven, and our yard holes are exposed to both.
