Security fence

The yard is already fenced, but with industrial chain link. Outdoor holes, props, furniture and the truck court sit out overnight, so the plan assumes a new, harder-to-climb perimeter that also looks good from the street.

Fence length: the lot is about 33,500 SF. A roughly 150 × 224 ft lot has about 750 ft of perimeter; the building wall covers part of it, leaving about 600 linear feet to fence. Confirm with the survey.
