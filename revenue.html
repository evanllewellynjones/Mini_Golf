How the model works

  • Golf = players per day × days open × price, separately for daytime and night.
  • Bar = golfer bar spend + bar-only guests (people who come for drinks and don't play).
  • Food truck fees are what trucks pay us per day; their food sales are theirs.
  • Prices are net of sales tax. Bear and bull presets bracket the base case.
Planning estimates, not quotes. Numbers on these pages feed the Excel P&L (806-Ramseur-PnL.xlsx), which is the source of truth for the model. Not legal, tax, or zoning advice.
