Concept

18 holes split across the site: 9 under the building roof so play survives rain and July heat, and 9 in the fenced yard for evening play under lights. A bar takes the remaining interior space and opens to the yard. An ice cream window serves the afternoon family crowd. Food trucks rotate in the yard, so there is no kitchen to build or staff.

Day and night

  • Afternoons: families, birthday parties, ice cream, lower golf price.
  • Nights: adults, bar-led, higher golf price, near Durham's bar cluster.

The Ramseur corridor has been converting from industrial to mixed use. Nearby: the former Canada Dry bottling plant at 506/608 E Ramseur is now Durham Bottling Company co-working, and 618 E Ramseur became a mixed-use office/retail building with a rooftop.

The seller, CityPlat, has also acquired 810 E Ramseur next door. Ask whether it could be part of the deal later for parking or expansion.

Buying is cheaper per year and you keep the land, but it ties up ~$540,000 of equity and you carry the zoning risk. A lease with an option to buy would let you prove the concept first. The lease figure excludes landlord approval for the use change.
