Concept

18 holes split across the site: 9 under the building roof so play survives rain and July heat, and 9 in the fenced yard for evening play under lights. A bar takes the remaining interior space and opens to the yard. An ice cream kiosk on the patio serves the afternoon family crowd. Food trucks rotate in the yard, so there is no kitchen to build or staff.

Listing photos: Crexi listing

Day and night

  • Afternoons: families, birthday parties, ice cream, lower golf price.
  • Nights: adults, bar-led, higher golf price, near Durham's bar cluster.

Buying is part of the thesis: the Ramseur corridor is converting from industrial to mixed use, and owning the land captures that. Nearby, the former Canada Dry bottling plant at 506/608 E Ramseur is now Durham Bottling Company co-working, and 618 E Ramseur became a mixed-use office/retail building with a rooftop. The seller, CityPlat, also owns 810 E Ramseur next door.
